The performance “She asked me if I knew that glitter used to be made of glass” is based on research on light and drag performance that started in the performance “On the edge of the world/light: Shimmering” shown in Gallery Miroslav Kraljević GMK at the beginning of 2025.

Three drag performers perform a fiction about lightkeepers and lamplighters. Using various light-bodies that they embody and put in relation to their bodies and costumes. A pile of glitter is accumulating in the corner, they shine or not depending on your position, and on the sources of light that are reflected from them, some visible, some not. Through the accumulation of text, light-bodies, costumes and elements in space, we build a narrative in which three drag performers search, exchange speech and lip-sync and perform for each other and the others present a story about light and darkness, glitter and shine.
In this drag performance, we use elements of club performance and forms created as resistance and celebration of queer identity in the community.

Performed by nominis, Mx. Bodliere, ONA/GRDA
Photos: David Bakarić

nominis studied MA New Media at the Academy of Fine Arts in Zagreb and participated in WHW Academy 2024. program. They work in 3D animation, new media, visual art, text and performance. In their practice they create a drag alter ego "Nominis" and work on/through its materialization in objects and digital space through video, installation and drag performance.
Nomi's latest solo exhibitions are: performance “On the edge of light(nes)s: Glittering” in GMK Zagreb, performance "THIS IS (W)HERE I POST FROM" in Gallery Kamba in Zagreb, Nominis 0.00# in Gallery VN and Nominis 0.123# in the gallery of Klub Kocka in Split.
They have exhibited at several group exhibitions in and out of Croatia, some of which are: "An Archive, a Body, an Album, and a Story" at Exhibit Eschenbachgasse in Vienna,"Koštac/grappling" at the improspections festival at MSU Zagreb, and "7 Faces of the City" at the Kortil Gallery in Rijeka.
