Jakob Wittkowsky
Sound Feeding the Soul like Fertilizer

Acting is most often evaluated based on what we manage to produce or finish and such understanding is corroborated by foundations built towards progress and infinite production. however, in an overload of speed, man-made kitsch and glossy violence, these foundations and construction can no longer be used as a basis for something we will keep relying on. The making of new protocols, as well as those we already act within, defines more and more obviously the way a body is supposed to move and what its actions should become to be recognised as valuable. That is why Jakob in his work starts from a place where this predicted movement is stopped and transformed, destroyed and where space opens for new forms of movement. Jakob is a performer who doesn’t prioritise building fixed forms; rather, by multiplying he is attempting to intervene into the performance space differently every time. The presence of disobedience during a performance always provides a focused construction of a possible existence in a creatively built system that nevertheless knows its soft limits and decisions well enough to open the field of play. In his works, Jakob calls for an active observation, offering responsibility. First and foremost, Jakob is a performer who maintains his inner world unequivocally and subtly immerses all the present parties deep into his world, where he reveals the capability not to impose a predefined order to chaos and entropy, but instead to develop a system which can remain open and which is subject to constant change and turmoil.

Dora Brkarić

 Sound Feeding the Soul like Fertilizer is Jakob's new work in progress that is going to be performed in Zagreb for the first time. Jakob will be sharing the material that takes the notion of ‘duty’ as a starting point and seeks to explore it from various different angles and in different arenas. How does the principle of duty shape the relationships one has with their surroundings, with people, with one's self, with a cause, with the state, with faith? Rather than trying to propose rational or logical responses, the work seeks to embrace the obscure and grotesque qualities that lie within body and self.

In association with Močvara Gallery

Choreography and Performance: Jakob Wittkowsky
Artistic Advisors: Friederike Kötter, Raoni/Muzho Saleh
Much thanks to Jakoozi Amsterdam

Jakob Witkowsky is a dancer, choreographer and musical artist born in Munich, Germany and currently based in Amsterdam, The Netherlands. Through working with movement, objects, sound, voice, text and improvisation, the work is exploring the malleability of the self, in structures of power and myth. With a background in circus and classical music, Jakob first worked as a social worker and in trade fair construction before coming to theater, initially working as assistant director. He graduated from choreography studies at the School for New Dance Development (SNDO). Jakob presented his work at Spider festival, Les urbaines, Rakete and many more.
