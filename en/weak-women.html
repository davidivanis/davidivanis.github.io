Eva Priečková & Zuzana Žabková
Weak Women

In the Weak Women dance performance, we emancipate weakness, fatigue, pain and exhaustion. We try to look at these states as resources that help us shape the work. To embody our idea of a contemporary woman who tries not to pretend and calm down the situation. On the contrary, she looks for excitement in moments of suffering and exhaustion.

Two female fossils clinging to their feet until the last moment.
The moment before exhaustion equals the moment before ecstasy.
We are out of energy at 11am.
In a state of constant exhaustion, we get down on our knees and bend our limbs into various shapes.

Towards the female ancestors, to the gestures, tics and nervousness that we have stored in our memory from our grandmothers and from photos of Slovak national uprising women. Petrified and stiffened bodies participate in the dance marathon of the precarious profession of "independent" artists in Slovakia. We can dance for decent working conditions, for the right to rest, for the involvement of art organizations in global issues.

We are broken and wounded, yet we never stop searching for playfulness, sensuality and pleasure.

Concept and performance: Eva Priečková, Zuzana Žabková
Music: Antonia Beeskow
Costumes and set design: Nik Timková, Vojtěch Hlaváček
Production: CO.LABS
Thanks to: Jakub Juhás – mappa editions, REZI.dance Komařice, SNG – Schaubmarov mlyn, Pezinok

Eva Priečková is an interdependent dance artist, researcher, and curator, primarily working around the residency and artistic research centre Telocvičňa in Bratislava. As Eva Priečková / False Move, she hosts the educational platform SOFT CORE, which brings together embodied movement practice with critical reflection on the body and relationality. Her work explores dance in diverse communities—including older adults, Roma communities, and social work contexts—as well as ecosomatics, more-than-human relations, and rituals of grief and celebration. She is a co-curator of the international symposium Contaminations. Through her artistic and research practice, she investigates the potential of dance education to foster social justice, mutual responsibility, and emancipation from the unconscious repetition of normative patterns of behaviour. For her, dance is an act of resisting and provoking docile bodies and minds through mutual listening, improvisation, awe and sensuality, weirdness and imaginations and through everything that cannot be expressed in words. In 2026, she completed her PhD at the Janáček Academy of Performing Arts (JAMU) in Brno. She deeply values collaboration and has worked for many years with Yuri Korec and Co., Zuzana Žabkovová, Katarína Poliačiková, Sonja Pregrad, and many other inspiring artists.


Zuzana Žabková is an artist, dancer, and choreographer exploring the body as a prophetic apparatus, always in touch with something beyond itself. Drawing from wounds, weakness, and brokenness as holy strategies for survival, she works with somatic fiction and collective play structures as laboratories for new forms of social relations. She is the co-founder of björnsonova, a platform, fictional character, community, and multifaceted dance form spanning various temporal, spatial, and artistic dimensions. She holds an MA from the Institute for Applied Theatre Studies in Gießen and is currently pursuing a PhD at the Faculty of Visual Arts in Brno. She lives and works between Frankfurt am Main and Prague.
